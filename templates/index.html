Architectural Framework

Four Core Intelligent Modules

Module 01
NLP Classification

Categorizes reports into IT, Electrical, Sanitation, Water, and Infrastructure domains using supervised machine learning algorithms.

Module 02
Priority Grading

Evaluates safety risks, structural failure, and campus disruptions to grade tickets as High, Medium, or Low urgency.

Module 03
Duplicate Merging

Computes Cosine Similarity across unresolved records, uniting duplicate reports from multiple students into a consolidated case.

Module 04
Recurrence Audit

Identifies chronic facility breakdowns at specific campus locations to recommend permanent capital replacement.
